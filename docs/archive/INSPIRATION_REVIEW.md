# Sources informing the strategy agent

Read November 2, 2026 for Hatem's requested inspiration review. These are design implications, not claims that the prototype reproduces the cited systems. Reading covered the linked pages, paper methods/evaluation sections, the supplement's assistant/evaluation descriptions, repository documentation, and relevant evaluator/distillation entrypoints. It was not a complete audit of all repositories or notebooks.

| [BoxingGym repository](https://github.com/kanishkg/boxing-gym) | Its environment/goal/agent boundaries offer a model for small controlled behavior diagnostics with reproducible experiment outcomes. | It is a scientific simulation benchmark. Our open strategy dossiers lack defensible likelihoods, so we should not manufacture a Bayesian reward or interpret information gain as business value. Keep SFT as the present pilot. |

## Changes recommended now

1. Preserve AgentTune and the guarded tools. Show the problem, rival causal theories, evidence links, proposed/declined tests, and revision history as explicit artifacts in the existing app.
2. Qualify the judge instrument through anchors, repeated ratings on a fixed subset, order/presentation checks and judge-level disagreement. Judge repetitions are not independent strategic cases.
3. Fix the effective context budget identically for B and C. Count context-limited and unfinished episodes in the primary all-case outcome; show reached-stage quality separately.
4. Separate teacher qualification from individual row screening. Mechanical validity alone cannot identify false inferences. A separately permitted, teacher-distinct quality screener needs a frozen criterion and a reliability check. Claude outputs/scores remain outside training-data selection under the current policy.
5. Treat a prediction-transfer diagnostic inspired by BoxingGym as a possible later addition. It is not required to finish the present baseline, teacher gate or SFT pilot.

## Questions resolved or left open

- A five-point per-theory belief scale is an ordinal within-run signal. It should not be conflated with probability of commercial success or confidence in an assessor's own estimate.
- Aya Expanse's weak strategy generation does not automatically disqualify it as a judge, but availability is insufficient: it must pass the same anchor/reliability gate.
- Claude's role in writing the rubric and reference is a potential source of bias. Disclose it, blind the judging invocation, include an independently authored anchor, and show whether conclusions depend on that judge.
- The held-out company/product/mechanism roster and approved teacher are still unresolved. These sources do not fill those gaps automatically.
