- **recommendation:** Invest in a differentiated consumer proposition targeting home barista enthusiasts, leveraging premium positioning and direct-to-consumer channels. Begin with a limited launch of a compact, high-quality espresso machine and build brand awareness through specialty retailers and social media.
- **target customer:** Home barista enthusiasts who enjoy coffee at home for social and personal enjoyment, and who value quality and brand.
- **value proposition:** A premium, compact espresso machine that combines professional performance with a user-friendly design, positioned as the ultimate home barista tool for personal enjoyment and social sharing.
- **favoured theory:** T1

- **uncertainty:** The long-term sustainability of home barista culture and consumer willingness to pay a premium for espresso equipment remain uncertain. The test will clarify whether demand is sufficient to justify investment.
