- **recommendation:** Invest in a differentiated consumer proposition targeting DIY homeowners and small businesses, leveraging Hach’s brand trust and existing distribution network. Begin with a limited pilot of a branded home testing kit to validate demand before scaling.
- **target customer:** DIY homeowners and small businesses seeking to ensure water safety.
- **value proposition:** A branded home water testing kit that combines Hach’s reputation for reliability with convenient, at‑home testing, enabling consumers to make informed decisions about water quality without lab visits.
